Throughline

Every assembly in a build, and what's holding it up, on one screen.

Break a product down into its subassemblies and see each one's kit, work order and work-plan steps in a single flow diagram. Throughline pulls in data from the systems you already use, so planners, engineers and technicians all look at the same picture.

"Throughline" is a working name.

  • The whole build at a glance

    Switch between a flow diagram, an indented parts list and a status board. Click any assembly to see its kit, work order and steps.

  • Know what's late, and why

    The critical path shows which shortage or hold is setting your finish date, so you know which problem to fix first.

  • Trace any part or lot

    Search a lot number and see every unit it went into, including units that have already shipped.

  • Works with your systems

    Designed to pull from ERP, PLM and supplier tools, starting with a simple spreadsheet import so nothing waits on IT.

The screen above is a concept with made-up sample data. Do you build hardware in-house? I'd like to hear how you track it.
