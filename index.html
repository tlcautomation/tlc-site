Good questions

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. If you can send a text or snap a photo, you can use what I build. I design around the tools and habits you already have.

Will you replace my software?

Usually not. Most of the time I work with what you already have, like QuickBooks, your email and your calendar, and make it do more for you. If something new would genuinely help, I'll explain why before suggesting it.

Can't I just use ChatGPT?

For answering questions, sure. But on its own it won't text back your missed calls, put receipts into QuickBooks or send invoice reminders while you're on a job. That takes connecting AI to your other software and setting it to run on its own, which is what I do.

Can't I just build it myself with AI?

Sometimes, yes. AI coding tools are now good enough that a simple personal tool can be a weekend project, and if that's all you need, I'll tell you. It gets harder when the tool touches your customers, your money or QuickBooks. Then someone has to understand the code, handle your data carefully, and fix it when it breaks. That's the part I take on, and it's all documented so you're never stuck.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what we build. After a free conversation, you get a fixed quote before any work starts, so there's no hourly meter running and no surprise bill.

What happens to my data?

It stays yours. I build on paid business plans from established AI providers, whose terms say your data isn't used to train their AI models, and I'll show you exactly where everything lives.

What if it breaks, or my needs change?

That's what the care plan is for. Tell me what's off and I fix it. Without a plan, you can still reach out for one-off changes, quoted up front.
